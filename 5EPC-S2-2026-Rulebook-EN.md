# Preface

This Rulebook sets out the rules and requirements that all participating teams and players must comply with in the course of participating in the 5EPlay Professional Challenge Season 2 (hereinafter "5EPC S2 2026" or "the Event"), with the aim of safeguarding fair competition, promoting sportsmanship and maintaining the integrity of the Event. Any violation of this Rulebook may result in the corresponding sanctions set out herein.

Players, substitutes, coaches, team managers and other team staff, upon registration or participation, shall be deemed to have fully read, understood and agreed to be bound by all provisions of this Rulebook.

The Tournament Management shall have the final authority over all matters of the Event. In respect of special circumstances not expressly enumerated in this Rulebook, or where it is genuinely necessary to safeguard competitive fairness and the integrity of the Event, the Tournament Management shall be entitled to make supplementary rulings in accordance with the principles of fairness and impartiality, and may, in exceptional circumstances, depart from specific provisions of this Rulebook. All rulings of the Tournament Management on disputes shall be final, and no repeated appeal on the same matter shall be accepted.

5EPlay is committed to presenting a high-quality, fair, exciting and mutually respectful esports event for players, viewers and partners, and thanks all participating teams for their participation.

## III. Overview of Stage Formats

**Open Qualifier (Online)**: Open registration, closed when full (registration cap: 64 teams). This stage shall be played in a single-elimination format; the first round shall be best-of-one (BO1), and all remaining matches shall be best-of-three (BO3); a total of 8 teams shall ultimately advance to the Group Stage. Teams advancing from the Open Qualifier and VRS direct-invitation teams enter the Event at the same stage (the Group Stage).

**Group Stage / Main Event Stage (Online)**: The 16 teams shall be divided into two groups, A and B, with 8 teams in each group, and each group shall be played in a double-elimination format; all matches shall be BO3. In each group, the 2 teams winning the upper-bracket semifinals and the 2 teams winning the lower-bracket semifinals shall advance, giving 4 teams per group and 8 teams in total advancing to the Playoffs. All VRS direct-invitation teams start from this stage; teams with a higher VRS ranking shall not enjoy any privilege of delayed entry or a bye.

**Playoffs (Online)**: The 8 teams shall compete in a single-elimination format. Except for the Grand Final, which shall be BO5 (best-of-five), all matches shall be BO3 (best-of-three).

# 1  General Provisions

## 1.1  Rule Agreement

This Rulebook is the governing document regulating the relationship between the organizer of 5EPC S2 2026 and all participants. Upon registration or participation, participants shall be obliged to familiarize themselves with these rules on their own initiative and may participate in the Event only after agreeing to all provisions.

## 1.2  Event Organization

5EPC S2 2026 is a fully online Tier 2 event organized by 5EPlay and featuring Counter-Strike 2. The Tournament Management comprises the Tournament Director, the Referee Team, the Operations Team, the Proctoring and Anti-Cheat Team, the Broadcast Team and other authorized staff, collectively referred to as the "Tournament Management" or the "Officials".

## 1.3  Event Management

### 1.3.1  Compliance with Instructions

Players and team staff shall at all times comply with the instructions and arrangements of the Tournament Management. Refusal, delay or obstruction of the execution of official instructions may result in penalty points up to and including disqualification.

### 1.3.2  Decisions and Appeals

The Tournament Management shall make all on-site decisions pursuant to these rules and may, where necessary, escalate matters level by level to the Tournament Director. Any party objecting to a ruling made during a match shall, through the team captain on the server, submit a written appeal through official channels within 30 minutes after the conclusion of the match, attaching evidence such as the specific round timestamp, a demo or screenshots; appeals submitted out of time or by anyone other than the captain shall not be accepted. Pending resolution of the appeal, the original on-site ruling and match result shall remain in force. The determinations of the Tournament Management on disputes shall be final.

### 1.3.3  Communication Channels

The official communication channels during the Event include: the match rooms on the 5EPlay competitive platform, the official operations group 【official voice channel, as announced before the event】, the official designated voice channel, and individual communication between the captain (manager) and the Tournament Management. Outside these channels, oral agreements made on other social software shall have no effect. Each team shall designate one captain as its primary contact and shall ensure that the captain is online and reachable by the Officials at all times during matches.

### 1.3.4  Authority of the Tournament Management

In order to make the competition as fair as possible, the Tournament Management shall be entitled to amend or supplement the applicable rules above and to rule on situations not expressly covered by the rules; the Tournament Director shall be entitled to overturn any prior on-site statement or decision.

### 1.3.5  Privacy and Recording

Without the permission of the other party and the Officials, players are strictly prohibited from privately recording, disseminating or publishing voice communications between players and the Officials, or between players and opponents (violators shall be subject to a long-term suspension). The Officials may, in accordance with the rules, record the official voice channels, match rooms and proctoring screens as required for event management, refereeing, proctoring, disciplinary investigations and compliance archiving; the specific scope of retention is set out in Article 6.9.

### 1.3.6  Match Times

Match times shall be subject to the official schedule and operations notices. In the event of force majeure or similar circumstances, the Officials shall be entitled to adjust or postpone match times and to reschedule in the manner most conducive to the complete conduct of the Event.

## 1.4  Rule Changes

The Organizer reserves the right to modify these rules without prior notice and reserves the right to determine matters not expressly covered by the rules. All rule changes shall be announced simultaneously through the official website and the public GitHub repository, and the latest version shall prevail.

## 1.5  Confidentiality

Information within match channels and all communications with the Event Officials shall be confidential information. Without the written consent of the Tournament Management, disclosure of such content to third parties is strictly prohibited, including but not limited to unpublished schedules, matchups, veto intentions, investigation processes and disciplinary discussions.

## 1.6  Match Broadcast and Spectating Restrictions

5EPlay owns the broadcast rights to all matches of the Event. Participating teams shall not refuse broadcast of their matches by officially authorized channels, nor may they choose the manner in which their matches are broadcast; whether to broadcast shall be determined solely by the Tournament Management.

- Without the written permission of the Officials, participating teams and players shall not stream their own matches, nor provide the match server, room password or spectator slots to any third party.
- **Stream Sniping is strictly prohibited**: During a match, players shall not watch any live stream, delayed stream or third-party perspective of their own team or of the match in question, and shall not obtain match information through any external source of information; violators shall be dealt with under the cheating-related provisions of Article 5.2.
- Players shall not deliberately evade the camera or delay the broadcast by means of delay, lag, disconnection or similar during official broadcasts.

## 1.7  Sponsorship

Team sponsors or partners shall not be associated with pornography, gambling, contraband or other content that may damage the reputation of the esports industry or the Organizer; displays involving betting or contraband-related sponsorships shall require separate written consent from the Officials and shall comply with applicable laws and regulations.

## 1.8  Team Standards

Teams and players shall, upon official request, truthfully and promptly provide the necessary registration and verification information.

### 1.8.1  Team Responsibility

Teams shall be responsible for the conduct of their players, substitutes, coaches, managers and other team staff; where such persons violate these rules, the team may be subject to joint sanctions. Teams shall cooperate with reasonable arrangements made by the Officials for the smooth start of matches, broadcasting and proctoring.

### 1.8.2  Team Names

Team names shall not carry undeclared extended suffixes and may contain only the team name and a permitted title sponsor; team names shall not contain political, religious, insulting or other unlawful, non-compliant or public-order-offending elements. The Officials shall be entitled to require players to use in matches a nickname consistent with their registration or matching official records.

### 1.8.3  Team Logos

Other than lawful title sponsors, team logos shall not contain brands, teams or trademark identifiers unrelated to the team, nor shall they contain political, religious or insulting elements. Team logos shall be submitted together with registration and pass review.

## 1.9  Prizes

Prizes shall be paid to the teams' corporate accounts within 30 business days after the conclusion of the Event in accordance with the standards set out in "Event Overview". If a winning team fails to provide payment information, invoices or other required materials promptly and accurately, the prize payment time shall be extended accordingly until the relevant issues are resolved. Prize taxes and fees shall be borne by the winning teams; beyond the prize pool, the Event offers no other compensation.

## 1.10  Voice Communication

Online matches shall uniformly use the officially designated voice software and channels 【Official voice channel, as announced before the event】, and the Officials shall be entitled to require a change. In each match's official voice channel, only the 5 players on the server and 1 registered coach may be present (coach communication restrictions are set out in Article 2.14); substitutes and other team staff shall enter a separate waiting channel and may enter only with official permission. Players shall enter the voice channel under their registered nicknames.

## 1.11  Eligibility and Suspension Standards

The following eligibility standards shall apply uniformly and transparently to all participants in the Event, with no discretionary exceptions:

- Players with a valid VAC ban record within 5 years prior to the start date of the Event shall not be eligible to participate.
- Players who receive a VAC ban after participating in any Valve VRS circuit event (including its qualifying stages) shall not be eligible to participate.
- Players or teams currently serving an ESIC suspension or with a verified match-fixing record shall not be eligible to participate.
- Players or teams permanently banned on a third-party competitive platform for integrity issues shall not be eligible to participate.
- Players whose VAC ban record is more than 5 years old and who have no other violations thereafter shall not be subject to participation restrictions.

**Non-exclusivity Principle**: The Organizer does not enter into any agreement with any team or player that restricts their participation in other events, and there are no exclusive arrangements in this Event.

**Non-discrimination Principle**: All participants at the same stage shall receive equal treatment in technical support, preparation time, rule enforcement and all other respects, and the prize and compensation structure shall be entirely identical for invited and qualifying teams.

**Conflict of Interest Disclosure**: There exist no business relationships, shared management, shared shareholding, authorization or licensing relationships, loans of funds or other interest relationships requiring disclosure to Valve between the Event operator and its staff and any participating team.

## 1.12  Registration, Roster Lock and Substitutions

### 1.12.1  Registration and Slots

Each team shall consist of 5 starting players and up to 2 substitutes; **each team may register a maximum of 2 substitutes in this Event**; all players shall hold a 5EPlay account that is in normal status, free of adverse records and usable. Public registration shall be completed by creating a team on the 5E competitive platform and submitting a registration; the order of registration shall be based on the registration timestamp recorded by the platform and shall be publicly displayed in real time in a public read-only spreadsheet, verifiable by anyone. After registration, team verification materials shall be submitted to the official email address 【Official registration / verification email, as announced before the event】 within 48 hours; slots shall not be retained for those who fail to submit within the deadline. The public registration cap shall be 64 teams; those exceeding the cap shall enter the waitlist in the order of submission.

### 1.12.2  Roster Lock

The entire roster (including up to 2 substitutes) shall be registered and submitted before the Roster Lock date of November 30, 2026; following the lock, changes shall in principle not be permitted. Initial seeding for teams registered for the Open Qualifier shall be determined on the basis of the November 2026 VRS ranking; a roster without a global VRS ranking shall not be seeded higher than a roster with a VRS ranking.

### 1.12.3  Original Roster Requirements for Directly Invited Teams

Each VRS direct-invitation team shall, in every match of this Event, field at least 3 players who were on the roster at the time the direct invitation was originally obtained; a team unable to satisfy the requirement of "at least 3 originally invited players per match" shall forfeit that match directly.

### 1.12.4  Substitutes and Emergency Stand-in (Stand-in)

Routine substitutions may be made only between a starting player and that team's registered substitutes (a maximum of 2). An emergency stand-in from outside the roster may be requested only where genuine emergencies such as network disconnection, illness or injury, or equipment damage occur and the registered substitutes are also unable to play: the request shall be submitted to the Tournament Management and approved before the scheduled start of that match, the emergency stand-in shall satisfy all eligibility requirements of the Event and shall not be a registered member of any other participating team; a player may represent only one team during the entire Event. Emergency stand-in situations shall be reviewed case by case by the Tournament Management.

## 1.13  Schedule, Check-in and Late Arrival

### 1.13.1  Schedule and Match Start

The Officials shall publish the schedule for each match day no later than the day before that match day. Teams shall complete check-in 30 minutes before the scheduled start time, enter the official voice channel and the proctoring meeting, and complete identity verification, anti-cheat and peripheral checks; the veto process shall commence 20 minutes before the scheduled start time (see Article 2.5 for details).

### 1.13.2  Rescheduling

All matches shall in principle start at the scheduled time. Where rescheduling is genuinely necessary, the captain or manager shall contact the Officials no later than 24 hours before the scheduled start of that match, and the Officials shall determine whether the reasons are valid and confirm the feasibility of rescheduling; any unilateral rescheduling without official confirmation shall be invalid.

### 1.13.3  Late Arrival and Non-appearance (Online)

Based on the scheduled start and veto times, the following progressive measures shall apply to teams that are not in position on time:

- If a team is still not in position 5 minutes after the veto has begun, 3 penalty points shall be imposed; if the match has still not started at the scheduled start time, a further 3 penalty points may be imposed for each additional 5 minutes of delay.
- If, 10 minutes after the scheduled start time, a team is still unable to begin the veto or the match, it shall lose the side-selection right, and the opponent shall select the starting side.
- If a team is still not ready to start 5 minutes after the scheduled start time, 3 penalty points shall be imposed, and the opponent shall be entitled to select the starting side.
- If a team is still unable to start 10 minutes after the scheduled start time, it shall be deemed to have forfeited: in BO1, the team shall forfeit the entire match; in BO3/BO5, the team shall forfeit the first map.
- If a team is still unable to start 30 minutes after the scheduled start time, it shall be deemed to have forfeited the entire series, and the opponent shall be awarded the official default win.

Teams shall explain the reasons for late arrival or non-appearance to the Officials; where the reasons are unjustified, additional suspension from this Event and even from subsequent events may be imposed. Inability to start on time due to a team's own network, equipment or account issues shall not constitute grounds for exemption.

## 1.14  Event Slots and Succession

VRS direct-invitation slots shall be allocated on the basis of the latest Valve official Asia VRS list as of the invitation date (November 20, 2026), with a "China" region filter applied, starting from No. 1 in the China region VRS ranking, for a total of 8 slots; where an invited team declines or fails to meet eligibility, slots shall pass down in order of VRS ranking until the 8 direct-invitation slots are filled, and a roster that has declined this invitation may not subsequently accept it. All directly invited teams must have a global VRS ranking outside the top 12 (excluding 12th place), so as to comply with Tier 2 event requirements.

The Open Qualifier shall produce the remaining 8 Group Stage slots, applicable to teams defined under Valve's official rules and policies as registered in China and with a relative majority of Chinese nationals among the 5 starting players; Open Qualifier seeding shall be determined on the basis of the November 2026 VRS ranking. The regional filter does not target any specific team or roster: among the Asian-region members of an invited roster, Chinese nationals must constitute a relative majority. This Event has no wildcard invitations. Where a team that has obtained a slot withdraws or is disqualified, the Officials shall determine the replacement on the basis of qualifying results or the order of alternates, and the Tournament Management reserves the final right of interpretation over replacement rules.

# 2  Match Rules

## 2.1  Game Version

The competition shall use the latest official release version of the Counter-Strike 2 client available at the start of the match. If the Officials determine that the latest version is unsuitable for competition due to a critical defect or an unforeseen pre-match balance update, the competition may revert to an available stable version; any update during the competition may result in a version rollback, and on-site notice from the Officials shall prevail. Players shall complete the client update themselves before the start of the match and may not delay the start of the match on that ground.

## 2.2  Match Settings

Matches shall use the MR12 format, with the first side to reach 13 points in regulation time winning. If regulation time ends in a 12:12 tie, overtime shall be played. Side selection shall be determined during the veto phase, and the starting side on the deciding map shall be determined by knife round. The specific server parameters are as follows:

| Setting | Parameter value | Server variable / notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular round limit | MR12, maximum 24 rounds | mp_maxrounds 24 |
| Round time | 1 minute 55 seconds | mp_roundtime 1.92 |
| Starting money | $800 | mp_startmoney 800 |
| Freeze time | 20 seconds | mp_freezetime 20 |
| Buy time | 20 seconds | mp_buytime 20 |
| C4 detonation timer | 40 seconds | mp_c4timer 40 |
| Tactical timeout | 3 per map × 30 seconds | Called by the teams themselves |
| Half-time break | 3 minutes (180 seconds) | Side switch at half-time |
| Round restart delay | 5 seconds | mp_round_restart_delay |
| Prohibited items | None | mp_items_prohibited "" |
| Overtime rounds | MR3, maximum 6 rounds | mp_overtime_maxrounds 6 |
| Overtime starting money | $12,500 | mp_overtime_startmoney 12500 |
| Overtime tactical timeout | 1 per team per overtime × 30 seconds | Unused regulation timeouts do not carry over or accumulate |
| Break during overtime | Disabled | No break between overtime halves |

## 2.3  Official Map Pool

The competition shall use Valve's current Active Duty map pool, with the baseline map pool as follows; if Valve updates the map pool before the competition, the official announcement shall prevail:

- de_inferno (Inferno)
- de_cache (Cache)
- de_anubis (Anubis)
- de_nuke (Nuke)
- de_mirage (Mirage)
- de_dust2 (Dust II)
- de_ancient (Ancient)

## 2.4  Tournament Format and Seeding

The Open Qualifier shall be single-elimination, and its initial seeding and the Group Stage seeding shall be determined on the basis of the November 2026 VRS ranking; teams without a global VRS ranking shall be seeded after teams with a VRS ranking. In the Group Stage, the 16 teams shall be divided into two groups, A and B, of 8 teams each, with double-elimination within each group, and all matches shall be BO3; in each group, the 2 teams that win the upper bracket semifinals and the 2 teams that win the lower bracket semifinals shall advance, for a total of 8 teams entering the Playoffs. The Playoffs shall be a single-elimination bracket, with all matches BO3 except the Grand Final, which shall be BO5.

In each match's veto, the higher-seeded team shall be entitled to choose whether to be Team A or Team B; seeding shall be determined on the basis of Group Stage placement / VRS ranking, and if the two teams hold the same placement, the team with the higher VRS ranking shall take precedence, and if they still cannot be distinguished, the Officials shall decide by drawing lots.

## 2.5  Veto Procedure

Online veto shall be conducted in a room designated by the Officials or in the match operations group, and each team may have only 2 members participate. Each participant's cumulative time for each ban or pick shall be 160 seconds; if no selection is made within the time limit, the Tournament Management shall decide at random on that participant's behalf. A ban/pick declaration made by a participant to the Officials shall take effect immediately and may not be withdrawn.

**BO1 (first round of the Open Qualifier)**: the higher-seeded team shall choose whether to be Team A or Team B, and then, in order:

1. Team A bans one map;
2. Team B bans one map;
3. Team A bans one map;
4. Team B bans one map;
5. Team A bans one map;
6. Team B bans the last map not to be played;
7. the sole remaining map shall be the match map, and the starting side shall be determined by knife round.

**BO3 (all other matches of the Open Qualifier, all Group Stage matches, and all Playoffs matches except the Grand Final)**: the higher-seeded team shall choose whether to be Team A or Team B, and then, in order:

1. Team A bans one map;
2. Team B bans one map;
3. Team A picks Map 1, and Team B picks the starting side on that map;
4. Team B picks Map 2, and Team A picks the starting side on that map;
5. Team A bans one map from the remaining maps;
6. Team B bans one map from the remaining maps;
7. the remaining map shall be the deciding map (if required), and the starting side shall be determined by knife round.

**BO5 (Grand Final only)**: the higher-seeded team shall choose whether to be Team A or Team B, and then, in order:

1. Team A bans one map;
2. Team B bans one map;
3. Team A picks Map 1, and Team B picks the starting side;
4. Team B picks Map 2, and Team A picks the starting side;
5. Team A picks Map 3, and Team B picks the starting side;
6. Team B picks Map 4, and Team A picks the starting side;
7. the remaining map shall be Map 5, the deciding map (if required), and the starting side shall be determined by knife round.

## 2.6  Match Servers

The competition shall use 5EPlay official online match servers (or servers of a platform designated by the Officials). After the veto procedure is completed, the Officials shall issue server information to both teams, and players shall complete connection at least 10 minutes before the start of the match. Server nodes shall be selected by the Officials on the principle of balanced and fair latency for both teams; if a team objects to the latency or the node, it shall raise the objection before the official start of the match (the end of freeze time in the first round), and after the match starts the server shall in principle no longer be changed on grounds of latency. If a match cannot proceed due to a failure of the official server, Article 2.8 shall apply, and such time shall not count toward the time limit for the team's own network failure.

## 2.7  Overtime

Overtime shall begin after a 12:12 tie in regulation time; overtime shall use MR3: 3 rounds per overtime half, with starting money of $12,500; teams shall begin overtime in the side they occupied in the second half of regulation time, and shall switch sides at the end of each overtime half. If an overtime ends in a 3:3 tie, the next overtime shall be played until a winner is determined. Each team shall receive 1 tactical timeout of 30 seconds per overtime; timeouts unused in regulation time shall be cleared upon entering overtime and shall not carry over or accumulate; no break shall be held during overtime.

## 2.8  Backup / Restore

When a match is interrupted for reasons beyond a player's control (such as an official server failure or a program crash not attributable to the player's own side), the referee may use CS2's built-in backup and restore functions to restore the round; in some circumstances, the Officials may decide to replay the round or the entire map:

- If the problem occurs before any damage or substantive engagement has taken place, and the opponent and the referee have been informed immediately, the round shall be restored to its initial state.
- If the problem occurs during the round, damage has been dealt, but the round outcome can still be determined (for example, only one player has been eliminated and the others remain alive), the round shall continue and count toward the result, with no backup and no replay; where the damage is minor and results from accidental friendly fire, or the affected side needs to rebuild the round, a special exception may be made.
- If the problem occurs during the round, damage has been dealt, and the round outcome can no longer be determined (for example, a server crash), the round shall be restored to its state at the beginning of the round.
- If the problem occurs during the round, damage has been dealt, and the course of the round is already clear (for example, one side has secured a weapon and health advantage), but the round cannot continue due to a server failure, the side most likely to win may be directly awarded the round.

If the incident is clearly attributable to a particular player personally (for example, an incorrect equipment purchase), the match shall not be stopped, and the round shall not be restored or replayed.

## 2.9  Breaks Between Maps

The standard break between maps in online matches shall be 10 minutes; in a BO5 series, the break after the end of Map 3 and before the start of Map 4 shall be 15 minutes, and all other breaks between maps shall be 10 minutes. For broadcast, technical, or operational reasons, the Officials may appropriately extend a break, and the extension arrangements shall be subject to official notice. After a break ends, players shall take their positions immediately; lateness shall be handled under Article 1.13.3.

## 2.10  Chat

In-game chat may be used only to communicate with the Tournament Management and to offer polite pre-match greetings to the opponent; posting advertisements or statements unrelated to the match before or after the match is strictly prohibited, and provoking the opponent or disrupting the match through chat is strictly prohibited. Where a backup, restart, technical timeout, or tactical timeout is required, it shall be requested to the referee via in-game chat or official voice. Chat violations may be punished by penalty points.

## 2.11  Timeouts

**Tactical timeout**: each team may request 3 tactical timeouts per map in regulation time, each not exceeding 30 seconds; upon entering overtime, remaining regulation timeouts shall be cleared, and each team shall receive 1 additional 30-second timeout per overtime. To request a tactical timeout, "!tac" shall be typed in the chat box (or the platform's timeout function shall govern), and it may take effect only during freeze time.

**Technical timeout**: a player may request a technical timeout when encountering a problem that prevents normal play, without limit on the number of requests. The request shall be made by typing "!tech" in the chat box with an immediate explanation of the reason; a technical timeout may be requested at any time, but shall take effect only at the next freeze time (that is, at the start of the next round). During a technical timeout, players may communicate only with the Tournament Management and may not discuss tactics with the coach or teammates (see Article 2.14 for coach rules).

## 2.12  Disconnections and Network Failures

Players shall be responsible for the stability of their own network and equipment. If a player disconnects, the captain shall request a technical timeout at the end of the current round, all players shall wait for that player to reconnect, and the disconnected player shall reconnect immediately.

- If a disconnection occurs while the round is live, the server shall continue to run; unless the backup conditions in Article 2.8 are met, no pause or rollback shall occur, and the engagement and results already produced shall remain valid.
- The disconnected player shall reconnect within 5 minutes; if the player cannot reconnect within that time, the Officials may rule that the match continue, that a compliant substitute take over, or, where continuation is impossible, that the team forfeit that map/match.
- Players may not intentionally delay the match by means of disconnection, restart, network switching, or similar; where a network cable, port, network, or device genuinely needs to be changed, the express approval of the Officials shall be obtained in advance.
- Where a team disconnects frequently, cannot stably complete the match, or refuses to cooperate with official network troubleshooting, the Officials may directly rule it to have forfeited.
- During a disconnection, no person who is not present in the match server may operate on the player's behalf, call out information, or give instructions; violators shall be handled as external assistance / cheating.

## 2.13  Player Settings

### 2.13.1  Configuration Files (Config)

All configuration adjustments may be used only where they do not provide an unfair advantage akin to cheating. If any configuration file of a player (even if not actually enabled and merely present in the game directory) contains prohibited settings, the player may still be penalized. A player who has doubts about the legality of any command or its value shall be obliged to confirm with the Officials in advance. All players shall submit their personal config for inspection at the time required by the Officials.

### 2.13.2  Scripts

All scripts are prohibited except buy scripts, toggle scripts, demo control scripts, r_cleardecals, and jumpthrow/runthrow scripts. Where a configuration file contains unauthorized scripts (whether or not in use), a penalty may be imposed.

### 2.13.3  Graphics Cards and Graphics Drivers

It is strictly prohibited to tamper with the game display through external graphics solutions, third-party software, or drivers in order to obtain an unfair advantage; violators shall be handled as cheating.

### 2.13.4  Overlays

Overlays displaying system or performance information within the game, such as Discord Overlay, Rivatuner, and the like, are strictly prohibited.

### 2.13.5  Device Drivers

It is strictly prohibited to pre-install or pre-program unauthorized macros through device drivers for keyboards, mice, sound cards, or similar; violators shall be handled as cheating (see also Article 6.7).

### 2.13.6  Prohibited Conduct

The following conduct is strictly prohibited during matches, and violators may cause their team to be disqualified:

- betting on or participating in gambling on matches of this competition;
- transmitting server data, room passwords, or spectator information to third parties;
- using scripts, "Bugs", game exploits, and map errors;
- moving through walls, floors, or roofs, and "Walking on the sky";
- installing programs that tamper with the display or skins, such as SweetFX and SkinChanger;
- planting the bomb in a position where the C4 cannot be defused (replanting is permitted, but using textures, walls, or similar to make it invisible or non-defusable is prohibited);
- using "Flash bugs";
- "Pixel walking": sitting/standing on invisible edge pixels of the map;
- using prohibited graphics settings such as 16-bit;
- using third-party software not authorized by the game publisher that may provide an unfair advantage (treated as cheating);
- the presence in the match server of strangers unrelated to the match;
- insulting opponents, teammates, the Officials, or other participants;
- conduct lacking sportsmanship, as determined by the referee on duty, including but not limited to watching the broadcast of the match and obtaining information from others (Tilt);
- account sharing, transfer, or having another person play on one's behalf.

### 2.13.7  Use of Game "Bugs"

Intentionally exploiting any "Bug", malfunction, or game error is strictly prohibited. Whether an exploit occurred, whether it affected the match, and whether a round/match forfeit shall be awarded or a replay arranged shall be determined by the Tournament Management. Errors expressly prohibited include: passing through areas of the map designed as impassable, planting C4 where the beep or planting sound cannot be heard, standing on a teammate's head to see through solid objects, pixel walking, interfering with map mechanics using prohibited objects (such as stopping the overpass train), money bugs from purchasing during freeze time, and the like; errors not listed shall be determined by the Officials on a case-by-case basis.

### 2.13.8  Steam Settings

Players shall disable Steam Overlay and set their Steam status to offline during the match; the match account shall be identical to the registered account.

## 2.14  Online Coaches

In official matches, each team may have 1 registered coach enter the official voice channel. A coach may communicate with players only during tactical timeouts, technical timeouts (when permitted by the Officials), and half-time breaks; during live rounds the coach shall remain muted, and calling out information, giving instructions, or transmitting any information is strictly prohibited, as is shouting loudly or emotional interference.

Where a coach violates the communication restrictions, the referee shall first issue a warning valid for the entire match; on a further violation, the coach shall be removed from the voice channel and suspended from coaching for the remainder of the match, and the match may be briefly paused for that purpose. Where the violation directly and significantly affects the match, aggravated penalties shall be imposed on the coach and the team together. Teams that repeatedly violate shall be given a prior warning, and after that prior warning the first violation may result in the coach being ejected directly. The Officials shall keep a record of coach warnings for review in subsequent disciplinary proceedings.

# 3  Online Participation Requirements

The competition shall be conducted entirely online. This chapter sets out the conditions that each team must prepare and satisfy itself in order to participate smoothly and compliantly; Chapter 6 sets out the integrity control procedures implemented by the Officials on that basis.

## 3.1  Own Equipment and Peripherals

Equipment shall not be provided uniformly for online matches; players shall provide and configure their own computers, monitors, keyboards, mice, mousepads, headsets, microphones, and network equipment, and shall ensure that such equipment can properly run the official match client, anti-cheat programs, and proctoring software. Where equipment is found in a pre-match inspection to present a security risk, contain prohibited functions, or possibly provide an unfair advantage, the Officials shall be entitled to require that it be disabled or replaced before the player may compete. Players may not request a delay of the match due to a malfunction or absence of their own peripherals; where a compliant substitute is available, it shall be used immediately.

## 3.2  Network Requirements and Responsibility

- Players shall bear the cost of network access and responsibility for its stability themselves; the use of stable wired broadband is strongly recommended, and contingency arrangements such as a mobile hotspot / backup network should be prepared.
- Any proxy, VPN, or unauthorized node of a network accelerator used to bypass anti-cheat, tamper with routing, or circumvent regional restrictions is prohibited; where a compliant acceleration tool is genuinely needed due to the network environment, it shall be declared to the Officials before the match and approval obtained.
- During matches, downloads, live streaming, streaming, and similar activities that occupy substantial bandwidth and may cause latency or packet loss shall not be conducted on the local network.
- Personal network failures shall be governed by Article 2.12 and shall in principle not constitute grounds for a pause, a replay, or exemption from liability.

## 3.3  Match Environment

- Players shall compete in an independent, enclosed, and quiet space; throughout the match, only the player registered to play in that match may be present in that space, and any other person is strictly prohibited from entering, watching, prompting, playing on another's behalf, or operating jointly.
- In principle, only one match monitor may be used; match-related information shall not be displayed on a second monitor, television, tablet, phone, or any other additional screen.
- Electronic devices such as phones, smartwatches, and tablets shall be set to silent and placed where the player cannot see or reach them during the match; they may be briefly used only when approved by the Officials for Steam Guard verification, and shall be put back immediately after use.
- The desktop and match area shall be kept tidy, and no paper, device, or screen capable of conveying match information shall be placed there.
- If the Officials require a camera to be enabled, the player's face shall be sufficiently lit and clearly visible, and the face shall not be obscured at any time by a hat, mask, headscarf, long hair, the headband of a headset, or similar; devices capable of playing external audio shall not be worn.
- A player's headset may be used only to receive game sound and official / team voice communication, and may not be used to receive any external communication; the Officials shall be entitled to require a player to show how the headset is worn or to adjust it.

## 3.4  Anti-Cheat and Platform Client

Players shall install, update, and run the 5EPlay official matchmaking platform (match client) and its anti-cheat components throughout the match as required by the Officials, and shall keep Valve VAC normally enabled. Players shall restart their computers and clients and close conflicting programs before the match as required. Disabling, uninstalling, bypassing, or interfering with anti-cheat programs, or using any means to render them ineffective, is strictly prohibited; violators shall be handled as cheating, shall be directly awarded a forfeit loss and disqualified, and may not refuse official anti-cheat inspections.

## 3.5  Identity Verification and Pre-Match Check-In

- The match account must be identical to the account registered for entry; borrowing or swapping accounts, account sharing, and playing on another's behalf are strictly prohibited; the Officials shall be entitled to conduct random verification of real names, faces, or identity documents at any time.
- Players shall enter the official voice channel and the proctoring meeting 30 minutes before the start of the match to complete check-in, and shall cooperate in completing roll call and screen and peripheral inspections.
- Match nicknames shall be consistent with the registered roster; the Officials shall be entitled to require a player to temporarily use a nickname in a specified format for identification purposes.
- A person who cannot pass identity verification, refuses verification, or is substituted by another person may not start the match, and where the circumstances are serious shall be handled as playing on another's behalf / cheating.

## 3.6  Permitted Software and Network Access

During official matches, only the following software may run: Steam, Counter-Strike 2, the voice software designated by the Officials, approved peripheral drivers, and competition / proctoring programs expressly authorized by the Officials. All other applications and background processes shall be closed unless specifically approved by the Officials. Players shall not:

- install or download any software without permission;
- use remote desktop, remote control, or remote assistance software;
- use virtual machines, emulators, Android subsystems, or multi-boxing / streaming environments;
- use third-party communication software to convey match information;
- use unauthorized overlays or performance monitoring software that displays additional game / system information;
- disable or interfere with security, monitoring, or anti-cheat system services;
- share the network with cheating devices, or connect to an external network or set up a mobile hotspot of one's own for other devices to obtain match information.

## 3.7  Proctoring: Screen Sharing, Cameras, and Screen Recording

- Players shall join the proctoring meeting as required by the Officials and, as required, share **all monitor screens** (including the taskbar, system tray, and a second screen, if any); sharing only the game window is not permitted.
- Where the Officials require a camera or a phone side camera to be enabled, the player shall set it up at the specified angle so that the player, both hands, the screen, and the desktop environment are simultaneously visible.
- Every player on every team shall make a **local screen recording** of their entire match process (including game video and system sound), and shall retain the recording for at least 7 days from the end of that match; when requested by the Officials, a clear, complete, and unedited original recording shall be submitted within 30 minutes.
- The Officials may randomly conduct inspections of the process list, background programs, peripherals, desktop environment, and camera before, during, or after the match, and players shall cooperate unconditionally.
- Refusing or interrupting screen sharing or the camera without proper reason, or refusing to submit a recording or submitting an edited recording, may result in penalty points, a forfeit loss, or even disqualification.

## 3.8  Compliance with Instructions and Cooperation with Inspections

Players and accompanying team personnel shall comply with all reasonable instructions issued by the Officials or their representatives. Failure to comply may result in penalty points; failure to cooperate with proctoring, anti-cheat, or an investigation shall be handled under the relevant provisions of Chapters 5 and 6 with aggravated penalties.

## 3.9  Media and Online Interviews

Players may not refuse a reasonable interview request from the Officials without proper reason; the Officials shall give advance notice of online media activities, and players may be required to provide material for the broadcast. Unexcused absence or refusal to cooperate shall be deemed a failure to fulfill media obligations and may result in penalty points. All participants authorize the organizer to use their likeness, match footage, and interview material within the scope of competition promotion.

## 3.10  Online Conduct and On-Camera Standards

Players shall conduct themselves in a civilized and disciplined manner in voice communication, chat, proctoring screens, interviews, and all public occasions related to the competition; insulting, threatening, sexist / regional / racial discriminatory, or other offensive language is strictly prohibited, as is provocation or incitement of hostility. Dress shall be neat and appropriate when on camera. Violators shall be punished in accordance with the relevant standards of Article 5.1.

# 4  Competitive Integrity and Anti-Corruption

## 4.1  Scope of Application and Responsibility

For the purposes of this Chapter, "Covered Person" means players, substitutes, coaches, managers, owners, analysts, staff, and any other person who represents or serves a participating team. Each team and its organisation shall take reasonable measures to ensure that all of its Covered Persons understand and comply with this Chapter. Any act of committing, soliciting, aiding, agreeing to, concealing, or conspiring to commit an integrity violation constitutes a violation even if the intended purpose is not ultimately achieved. After a Covered Person ceases to participate, their obligations of cooperation, evidence preservation, and confidentiality continue to apply within the scope of this Chapter.

## 4.2  Betting and Gambling

No Covered Person shall directly or indirectly engage in any of the following:

- placing or attempting to place a bet on any match, map, round, veto, score, player performance, statistic, or other related matter of the Event;
- instructing, encouraging, funding, or permitting another person to bet on their behalf;
- using another person's account, a shared account, a company account, or any intermediary account to place or conceal bets;
- receiving, sharing, or otherwise obtaining the proceeds of prohibited betting;
- using their role in the Event to provide access, assistance, advice, or information in order to facilitate betting.

The existence of a lawful, disclosed sponsorship, advertising, or commercial relationship with a betting operator does not in itself automatically constitute a violation, but such a relationship shall not be used to obtain Inside Information, influence competitive decisions, or gain any other improper competitive advantage.

## 4.3  Inside Information

"Inside Information" means any non-public information obtained through participation in or association with the Event that has not been made public and may not be used for betting, match manipulation, or other improper purposes, including but not limited to:

- map veto intentions or outcomes prior to official announcement;
- planned roster, substitute, or personnel changes;
- a player's illness, injury, absence, travel issues, or disciplinary status;
- technical failures affecting a team;
- team tactics or the content of confidential communications;
- unpublished schedule or format changes;
- protests, investigations, penalties, or administrative decisions not yet rendered;
- other information that may affect betting markets or competitive outcomes.

Covered Persons shall not use Inside Information to obtain market or other improper benefits, and shall not disclose it to unauthorised persons; disclosure is permitted only where genuinely necessary for the legitimate operation of the team or the Event, where required by the Officials, or where required by law. Once information has been lawfully and publicly disclosed, it no longer constitutes Inside Information.

## 4.4  Match Manipulation and Match-Fixing

All participants must compete to win with full effort and shall not deliberately underperform for improper or non-sporting purposes. Match manipulation and match-fixing mean improperly altering, by act or omission, any of the following: the result or course of a match or map; the outcome of a particular round, pistol round, or other in-game event; the final score or margin; the statistical performance of a player or team; the veto; seeding or playoff position; and any other matter on which betting markets or competitive interests may depend. Deliberately losing rounds, pre-arranging match results, giving an opponent an improper advantage, or coordinating an interruption of a match for financial or personal gain is strictly prohibited. Legitimate tactical decisions made purely to secure a team's competitive victory do not constitute match manipulation.

## 4.5  Bribery, Coercion and Improper Benefits

No Covered Person shall offer, promise, give, accept, or agree to accept money, gifts, services, favours, or other benefits in exchange for influencing the outcome of a match, disclosing Inside Information, breaching these Rules, or concealing a violation. Forcing another person, by threat, pressure, extortion, or similar means, to commit or conceal an integrity violation is strictly prohibited in all cases.

## 4.6  Conflicts of Interest

Covered Persons shall disclose any actual, potential, or perceived conflict of interest that could reasonably affect their independence, impartiality, or competitive decisions, including: holding shares or a financial interest in a competing team or organisation; sharing ownership, management, employment, or contractual relationships with a competing team; providing loans or guarantees to other participants or having employment or contractual relationships with them; having a material financial relationship with a betting operator; and having a close personal or family relationship with a participant or Event official that could reasonably affect impartiality. Conflicts of interest shall be disclosed in writing to the Officials before the roster deadline or immediately after the conflict arises; the Officials may require further information, adopt reasonable safeguards, restrict the Covered Person's duties, require a written waiver, or prohibit their participation where the conflict cannot be properly managed.

## 4.7  Mandatory Reporting

Covered Persons shall report immediately upon encountering any of the following: any contact, offer, request, threat, or inducement relating to betting or match manipulation; any request for Inside Information; any undisclosed conflict of interest; any known or reasonably suspected violation of this Chapter; and any attempt to conceal or destroy relevant evidence. Reports shall be made through official channels as soon as possible and no later than 24 hours after becoming aware; where the matter may affect an upcoming or ongoing match, it shall be reported before that match begins, and immediately if it is already in progress. Relevant messages, documents, recordings, and other evidence shall be properly preserved. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith or assists an investigation is strictly prohibited.

## 4.8  Investigations and Cooperation

In integrity investigations initiated or authorised by the Officials, Covered Persons shall cooperate fully, truthfully, and promptly, including by: attending interviews, answering relevant questions truthfully, providing relevant documents, communication records, logs, and account information, submitting to inspection of match devices and peripherals, and preserving devices or information that may reasonably be required. Any request must be reasonably relevant to the investigation and handled in accordance with law. Providing false or misleading information, refusing to cooperate without good cause, deleting or tampering with evidence, concealing information, colluding on testimony, obstructing an investigation, or retaliating against witnesses separately constitutes a violation. The Officials may inspect and analyse match computers, peripherals, server logs, demos, official voice recordings, and other data under official control, and may share them with Valve, other tournament organisers, integrity bodies, or competent authorities where necessary and lawful. Except as authorised by the Officials or required by law, all persons shall keep the existence and content of an investigation confidential.

## 4.9  Interim Measures and Sanctions

During an investigation, in order to protect the Event, the Officials may adopt interim measures proportionate to the circumstances: requiring the Covered Person to withdraw from competition, imposing a temporary suspension, restricting their access to Event systems or facilities, preserving devices or evidence, postponing a match, or temporarily withholding relevant prize money. Interim measures do not in themselves constitute a final finding of a violation. Depending on the nature and impact of the violation and the intent involved, final sanctions may include: a warning or penalty points; forfeiture of a number of rounds, maps, or matches; annulment of relevant results; disqualification; forfeiture of prize money; suspension or a ban on participation in future 5EPlay events; and referral to Valve, other tournament organisers, integrity bodies, or competent authorities. In order to eliminate an improper advantage and restore competitive fairness, competitive remedies may apply to the entire team even if not every player personally participated in the violation. Final disciplinary decisions shall state in writing the relevant violation and sanction and may be appealed under these Rules; unless the Officials decide otherwise, on-site rulings and interim measures remain in effect during an appeal.

# 5  Violations and Penalties

All participants shall respect representatives of the organiser, the media, spectators, partners, and other players. Where these Rules are violated, the Tournament Management may apply the appropriate penalty at its discretion.

## 5.1  Definition and Scope of Penalties

The forms of penalty in the Event include a warning, penalty points, forfeiture of a match, deduction of prize money, suspension of a player, coach, or team, and disqualification, which may be applied individually or in combination. Participants shall be notified of the penalty in writing and are entitled to the corresponding appeal period.

### 5.1.1  Penalty Points

Penalty points apply to competition-related violations. **Each 1 penalty point deducts 1% of the prize money ultimately awarded to that team**; for teams that receive no prize money, accumulated penalty points serve as the basis for escalating warnings, forfeiture of matches, and disqualification. A team may be given a maximum of 2 warnings; any subsequent violation is directly recorded as penalty points. The baseline penalty points for common violations are as follows:

- failure to comply with an instruction of the Officials: 1 penalty point;
- arguing with a referee: 1 penalty point;
- using insulting, threatening, or racially discriminatory language towards opponents, teammates, the Officials, casters, or others: 3 penalty points;
- conduct lacking sportsmanship (such as disrupting the normal conduct of a match): no fewer than 3 penalty points;
- publishing match information on public channels in advance: a warning or 1 penalty point;
- violations specific to online matches (failure to check in on time, refusing screen sharing or camera use, running unauthorised software, leaving the seat or disconnecting without permission, unrelated persons present in the match area, etc.): 1–3 penalty points depending on the circumstances; see Appendix D for the complete reference.

Where a match is subject to frequent delays, the Officials are entitled to impose penalties on their own initiative, for example by awarding the higher seed position to the opposing team during the veto.

## 5.2  Cheating

Any person found cheating by the game publisher's anti-cheat system (Valve Anti-Cheat, VAC), or who has a cheating record within 2 years prior to the Event, is ineligible to participate. The following programs are all deemed cheating (by way of example only and not limited to these): multihacks, wallhacks, aimbots, coloured models, no-recoil, no-flash, tampered sound effects, and exploitation of map bugs (such as sticking to invisible pixels or seeing players through textures); other programs or means may likewise be deemed cheating.

### 5.2.1  Cheating Software

The use of any software that may be deemed cheating is strictly prohibited, and the organiser reserves the right to determine the scope of what constitutes cheating.

### 5.2.2  Prohibited Communication and Stream Sniping

Communicating with persons not participating in the match in order to obtain information is strictly prohibited; watching a live stream of one's own match, or using any other external information source to obtain match information, is strictly prohibited.

### 5.2.3  Penalties for Cheating

Where the above violations are discovered during the Event, the relevant match results are annulled, the person concerned is disqualified, and no prize money is paid. The Officials are entitled to inspect players and their equipment by various means (including remote checks, anti-cheat back-end data, video review, proctoring footage, etc.) without prior notice, and players may not refuse inspection.

## 5.3  Doping

The use of controlled stimulant substances for the purpose of enhancing competitive performance is strictly prohibited, including but not limited to amphetamines (such as Evekeo), dexmethylphenidate/methylphenidate (such as Adderall, Ritalin, Concerta, etc.), dextroamphetamine (such as Dexedrine), lisdexamfetamine (Vyvanse), modafinil, and armodafinil. Refusing to undergo a doping test when required is treated as a violation of using a prohibited substance. Where a player requires medication containing the above substances for therapeutic purposes, the player must provide medical evidence to the organiser and declare it no later than two weeks before the start of the Event. All doping cases are determined and handled by the organiser on a case-by-case basis.

## 5.4  Alcohol and Drugs

Participating while intoxicated or visibly under the influence of other substances is strictly prohibited. Although there is no physical venue for online matches, players must remain sober and in normal competitive condition during matches; violators may be suspended or disqualified.

## 5.5  Betting

Players, coaches, managers, and team staff shall not participate in betting or gambling on the Event, collude with betting parties, or directly or indirectly provide any information for betting purposes. Betting on one's own team or on matches of one's own organisation results in suspension for all Covered Persons; betting on one's own match or organisation results in a minimum suspension of 2 years and 6 months from all 5EPlay events; other betting violations are determined by the Tournament Director at their discretion, with a minimum of disqualification from that event.

## 5.6  Match Manipulation

Any act of influencing others or swaying match results by means of bribery, threats, pressure, or similar means constitutes match manipulation. Violators have the relevant match results annulled and are disqualified and suspended.

## 5.7  Match-Fixing

Any team found to have engaged in match-fixing is immediately disqualified from the Event and suspended, with the suspension period determined by the organiser; where a criminal offence is suspected, the matter is referred to the competent authorities.

## 5.8  Uniform Application of Eligibility Suspensions

The eligibility suspension criteria set out in Article 1.11, including VAC bans, ESIC bans, and permanent bans on third-party platforms, apply uniformly and remain continuously in effect throughout registration, the qualifiers, the Group Stage, and the Playoffs. If a participant is found to be ineligible after the start of the Event, or if a VAC ban or similar circumstance arises during the Event, their eligibility and the results obtained are immediately annulled and no prize money is paid; there is no discretionary exception to the application of this Article.

# 6  Online Match Integrity and Environment Control

## 6.1  Definitions and Application

"Online Match Space" means the physical room in which a player participates, which is provided by the player but subject to official control, together with its computers, peripherals, screens, network, and software environment. The "Controlled Match Period" begins when the Officials close admissions and require players to enter a controlled state (no later than 15 minutes before the scheduled start of the match), and ends when the Officials formally release that match. Unless otherwise stated, the rules in this Chapter concerning match computers, peripherals, external devices, and software also apply to other computers, tablets, mobile phones, and similar devices within the player's participating space.

## 6.2  Access to the Online Match Area and Authorisation

Access to the Online Match Space in a controlled state and to the official voice/proctoring channels is limited to verified and authorised persons. During the Controlled Match Period, only the following are permitted in the match channel: 5 participating players, 1 registered coach, Event officials, and technical, broadcast, or medical personnel who have a genuine work requirement and are authorised. Substitutes, managers, analysts, content staff, and other team personnel must leave the match channel and the player's match space before the controlled period begins, or enter a separate waiting area as instructed by the Officials. Approved content personnel may capture material only within the scope designated by the Officials, and shall not record confidential screens or voice communications, interfere with players, or publish information that may affect the match. Refusing to leave or entering without authorisation constitutes a serious integrity violation.

## 6.3  Pre-Match Preparation and Technical Checklist

For each online match, each team must enter a controlled state at least 30 minutes before the start of the match, with both teams' preparation time being substantially equal. Pre-match preparation and checks include:

- connection, configuration, and registration of match peripherals;
- functional verification of computers, monitors, headsets, and microphones;
- verification of in-game audio/video/display settings;
- connection to the official match server and official voice server;
- network and latency testing;
- anti-cheat client and proctoring (screen sharing/camera) in place;
- closing the unauthorised programs listed in Article 3.6;
- pre-match warm-up.

Delays caused by a team's own reasons (late arrival, failure to submit configurations or materials on time, etc.) do not entitle that team to additional preparation time. Players must immediately report any problem discovered during the preparation phase; before the start of the match, each player and the captain must confirm that settings have been checked and that all known issues have been reported. This confirmation does not waive their right to report separately technical failures that were difficult to reasonably detect during preparation or that arise only during the match.

## 6.4  Match Equipment and Peripherals

Players' self-provided peripherals, including keyboards, mice, mousepads, in-ear headsets, and sound cards, must be subject to official inspection and approval. The Officials may refuse the use of, or require the temporary removal of, devices that: have not been registered and approved, cannot be properly inspected, present a technical or security risk, contain prohibited functions, or may confer an improper competitive advantage. Backup peripherals shall be kept outside the match space and may be used only with authorisation; players shall not delay a match on the grounds that their personal peripherals are unavailable or malfunctioning, and shall immediately switch to a compliant alternative where one is available. Without the permission of an Event official, players shall not modify system settings, or disconnect, reconnect, move, or interfere with match computers, monitors, audio equipment, network cables, power supplies, or other equipment.

## 6.5  Storage Devices, Virtual Machines and Remote Tools

Connecting personal storage devices to match computers, including USB flash drives, portable SSDs/HDDs, and memory cards, is strictly prohibited, as is running virtual machines, emulators, remote desktops, remote assistance, screen streaming, or any other environment that may introduce external information or input. Player configurations and required drivers must be submitted through official channels by the official deadline; any manual installation or configuration must be carried out under official supervision or remote verification. Match devices and login accounts are assigned to specific individuals and shall not be shared among players or connected to any unauthorised computer or device. Mobile phones may be used for Steam Guard verification only when approved, and must immediately afterwards be handed over for official remote verification and then returned to the designated location.

## 6.6  Permitted Software and Network Access

During official matches, only Steam, Counter-Strike 2, the official voice client, approved peripheral drivers, and Event/proctoring programs expressly authorised by the Officials may remain open or running; all other applications and background processes must be closed. Players shall not install or download unauthorised software, use remote access software, use third-party communication software, use unauthorised overlays or performance monitoring programs, or disable or interfere with security/monitoring/anti-cheat system services. Unless otherwise authorised by an Event official, players shall not access external networks unrelated to the match during the match, nor share their network connection with other devices.

## 6.7  SOCD, Macros and Automated Input

The use of "simultaneous opposite cardinal direction" (SOCD) functionality is strictly prohibited, including Snap Tap, Rappy Snappy, and any solution that, through hardware, software, firmware, drivers, or keyboard configuration, nullifies, suppresses, or automatically releases one directional input to produce an equivalent competitive effect. Unless expressly permitted by these Rules, it is strictly prohibited to use macros, multi-action bindings, or any function to automatically perform multiple in-game operations that a player is otherwise required to perform separately. Where a prohibited function is found during the pre-match inspection, the Officials may require it to be disabled, refuse the device, and conduct further investigation; where prohibited SOCD or automated input is detected as enabled during an official match, that team forfeits that map by default; in aggravated circumstances such as repeated violations or deliberate concealment, the team may directly forfeit that match or be disqualified.

## 6.8  Electronic Devices and Paper Materials

During the Controlled Match Period, mobile phones, all types of watches, tablets, laptops, audio/video recording devices, wireless headsets, and other unauthorised electronic devices shall be strictly prohibited from being present in a usable state within the player's match space; such devices shall be switched off or silenced and placed out of the player's sight and reach until that match is released by the Officials. Where a device genuinely needs to be used between maps, the player must leave the match station and obtain express official permission, and must return the device to its place before returning. Electronic hand warmers and medical devices may be used only after pre-match inspection and approval. Paper tactical materials, notebooks, and similar items may be used in reasonable quantities but are subject to inspection; coaches may view and use them only during timeout periods in which communication is permitted, and shall not convey information to players by gestures, displays, or other means during periods when communication is prohibited. Unnecessary bags, clothing, and items shall be kept outside the match space.

## 6.9  Voice Communication and Recording Retention

The official designated voice server is the only permitted voice communication tool during matches. Only the 5 participating players and 1 registered coach are permitted in the match channel, and players must join using their registered nicknames. Coaches may communicate only during the periods permitted by Article 2.14; during technical timeouts, communication between players and coaches is prohibited unless expressly authorised by an Event official. Players and coaches shall not communicate, by voice, text, gestures, signals, or any other means, with unauthorised persons during the Controlled Match Period. The Officials may record official voice communications for purposes such as Event administration, verification of coach restrictions, protest investigation, integrity and disciplinary investigations, and compliance with legal obligations; recordings are generally retained for up to 180 days from the end of the Event, and where a protest, investigation, payment dispute, or legal matter remains unresolved, the relevant recordings may be retained until the matter is finally resolved. Access to recordings is limited to authorised Event, integrity, and legal personnel, and recordings are not disclosed externally except with authorisation, consent, or as required by law, and are handled in accordance with applicable privacy laws and the organiser's privacy statement.

## 6.10  Leaving the Match Area and Reconnecting

During the Controlled Match Period, players shall not leave the match station, leave the camera frame, or exit the official voice or proctoring session without the permission of an Event official. Players shall not exit the match server, restart the game or computer, disconnect or reconnect peripherals, or change hardware connections. Players shall not voluntarily disconnect from the match server during a live round; reconnection is permitted only during a technical timeout and after express official approval. When a technical problem occurs, players must immediately notify an Event official and follow the official timeout procedure, and shall not resolve the matter themselves where software, cables, ports, devices, or network settings need to be changed. Where a brief absence is genuinely necessary, such as to use the restroom, it must be requested during a non-live period and approved, and the player may leave only after the Officials have recorded it and arranged a timeout, and must undergo verification again upon returning.

## 6.11  Remote Inspections

The Tournament Management may, without prior notice, conduct remote inspections of players themselves, peripherals, device configurations, firmware, configuration files, and match devices; inspections may include non-invasive security checks, verification of processes and background programs, screen-sharing inspections, and camera-based environment inspections, and shall be conducted within the limits permitted by applicable law. During an inspection, the Officials may temporarily seal relevant devices or files for further verification and require the player to continue the match using an approved replacement device. Any inspection of personal devices must be reasonably related to Event security or integrity and conducted in accordance with law. Refusing to cooperate with an authorised inspection without good cause may result in the device being prohibited, removal from the Controlled Match Space, or even disciplinary action.

## 6.12  Consequences of Violations

The Officials may postpone or refuse a team's entry into a match until the online integrity requirements are satisfied. Violations of this Chapter may result in a warning, penalty points, forfeiture of a number of rounds or maps, forfeiture of an entire match by default, removal from the match channel or proctoring session, or disqualification. Any conduct involving cheating, external assistance, unauthorised competitive information, or deliberate concealment shall also be handled concurrently under the competitive integrity rules in Chapter 4.

- BO3 (all other Open Qualifier matches, all Group Stage matches, and all Playoffs matches except the Grand Final): A ban → B ban → A picks map 1 (B chooses side) → B picks map 2 (A chooses side) → A ban → B ban; the remaining map is the deciding map (sides chosen by knife round).
